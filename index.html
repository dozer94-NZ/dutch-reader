Flashcards

10 cards. Tap a card to flip it, then swipe right if you got it, left if not. Missed cards come back at the end until you get them all. Retries earn nothing, and you need to finish the cards to bank what you won.

Type it

10 words: type the answer instead of picking it. Typing pays double. Capitals and accents don't matter, and one wrong letter is "Almost" and isn't counted.

Verb drill

10 questions on the irregular verbs: pick the right past tense or perfect.

⚔ Battles

You play 10 questions now. Your friend plays the same ones whenever they like. Highest score wins the pot: you set a wager (in tens), they match it, and the winner takes both. A draw gives the wagers back. "Anyone" is an open challenge: the first person to play it takes the wager, then it disappears.

Wager (each) €40

Handicap: only words neither of you knows yet, so nobody has a head start.
